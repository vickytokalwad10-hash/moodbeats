MoodBeats Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2025

1. Camera Access

MoodBeats requests access to your device camera solely for the purpose of face-based mood detection. Camera images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Data We Collect

MoodBeats does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All processing happens locally on your device.

3. Music & Playlists

Your music preferences and playlists are stored locally on your device only.

4. Third-Party Services

MoodBeats may load face detection AI model files from a public CDN (jsDelivr/GitHub). No personal data is sent in these requests.

5. Children's Privacy

MoodBeats is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
